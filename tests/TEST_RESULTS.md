## How to read the tolerances

* published benchmark tolerances cover the source's own rounding and the water properties used (the Holtrop example is quoted to 0.07 %, so 2 % is not a licence to drift — it is the paper's rounding plus a margin for ρ/ν);
* chart readings (B-series band, D/W) are read off a printed curve, so 4-10 % is the reading uncertainty, not the model accuracy;
* analytic/identity cases are exact to floating point (1e-9 relative or 1e-6 %);
* screen cases assert structure (a violation must never be presented as compliant), not a number.
